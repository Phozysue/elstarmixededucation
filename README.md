# Remix of School Site Builder

Here’s a **complete prompt** you can use on a **no-code or low-code website builder** like **Lovable.dev** to generate a **professional school website** with all key pages, admin and user functionality (students and teachers):

---

### 💡 **Prompt for Lovable.dev or any no-code/low-code platform**

**Prompt:**
Create a **modern, responsive, and professional school website** for **[School Name]** using a **no-code or low-code approach**. The website should include both **frontend pages** for visitors and **backend dashboards** for admins, teachers, and students.

---

### 🏫 **Website Overview**

The website should represent a **primary, secondary, or high school** (customizable). It must have an appealing, clean design with the school’s **colors, logo, and motto**, and it should be **mobile-friendly, fast, and secure.**

---

### 🌐 **Main Public Pages**

1. **Home Page**

   * Attractive hero banner with school motto, call-to-action buttons (“Apply Now”, “Contact Us”)
   * Quick links to About, Academics, Admissions, News, and Contact
   * Highlights of achievements, facilities, and testimonials

2. **About Page**

   * School history, mission, vision, and core values
   * Message from the Principal/Headteacher
   * School management team section

3. **Academics Page**

   * List of departments or classes (e.g., Kindergarten, Junior, Senior)
   * Curriculum details, subjects, and academic calendar
   * Downloadable timetables and syllabuses

4. **Admissions Page**

   * Step-by-step admission process
   * Online application form with file upload (birth certificate, transcripts, etc.)
   * Admission requirements and deadlines

5. **Gallery Page**

   * Photos and videos from school events, trips, and activities

6. **News & Events Page**

   * Latest school news, updates, and upcoming events
   * Ability for admin to post and edit announcements

7. **Contact Page**

   * Contact form (name, email, message)
   * Map integration and contact details (phone, email, address)
   * Social media links

---

### 👩‍🏫 **User Portals**

Create **separate dashboards** for:

1. **Admin**
2. **Teacher**
3. **Student**

#### 🧑‍💼 **Admin Dashboard Features**

* Manage student, teacher, and staff accounts
* Approve or reject admission applications
* Post news, announcements, and events
* Upload timetables and academic materials
* View overall academic performance and attendance reports
* Manage class schedules
* Upload photos to gallery
* Send messages or circulars to users

#### 👩‍🏫 **Teacher Dashboard Features**

* Manage assigned classes and subjects
* Upload and grade assignments and exams
* Track student performance and attendance
* Communicate with students and parents
* Share learning resources or notes

#### 🎓 **Student Dashboard Features**

* View personal profile, timetable, and grades
* Submit assignments and check feedback
* Access study materials and announcements
* Track attendance records
* Communicate with teachers or classmates

---

### 🔐 **User Accounts & Authentication**

* Role-based login (Admin, Teacher, Student)
* Secure password reset
* Profile picture upload and editable personal info
* Option for email or student ID login

---

### 🧰 **Additional Functionalities**

* School calendar integration (events, exams, holidays)
* Online fee payment or fee tracking system
* Notifications and messaging system
* Document management (report cards, certificates)
* Parent access (optional module)
* Search and filter for classes, teachers, or announcements

---

### 🎨 **Design Requirements**

* Professional, minimalistic, and education-themed layout
* Color palette matching school branding (e.g., blue, gold, or green)
* Use icons and imagery representing learning, growth, and technology
* Consistent fonts and responsive layout for all screen sizes

---

### 🧱 **Technical / Platform Requirements**

* Built using a **no-code or low-code platform (like Lovable.dev, Webflow, or Bubble)**
* Include database collections for users (students, teachers, admin), classes, events, and announcements
* Simple admin CMS for managing all content
* Automatic backups and secure data handling

---

### 🧾 **Footer Section**

* Quick links (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Contact)
* Social media icons
* Copyright © [Year] [School Name]. All rights reserved.
